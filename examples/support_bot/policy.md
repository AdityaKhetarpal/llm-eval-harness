VOLTKART CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
(Voltkart is a made-up online electronics store used to demonstrate evalgate.)

1. RETURNS
- Most products can be returned within 7 days of delivery, unused and in the original box.
- Mobile phones and laptops cannot be returned for change of mind. If they are defective, they can be replaced within 7 days of delivery.
- Earphones, chargers and cables: replacement only, within 7 days of delivery, if defective.

2. REFUNDS
- After a returned item passes inspection, the refund goes to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- UPI refunds usually arrive within 2 business days.
- Cash on Delivery orders are refunded to the customer's bank account within 7 business days. The customer shares bank details through the Voltkart app.

3. CANCELLATION
- An order can be cancelled free of charge before it is shipped.
- After an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled. The customer can refuse the delivery, or return the item under the returns policy.

4. DELIVERY
- Delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 have a ₹49 delivery fee.
- Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business days everywhere else.
- Express next-day delivery costs ₹99 and is available only in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru.

5. CASH ON DELIVERY
- Available for orders up to ₹50,000.
- Not available for gift cards.

6. WARRANTY
- Every product carries the brand's manufacturer warranty, usually 1 year.
- Voltkart does not repair products. For repairs, the customer contacts the brand's service centre.
- Voltkart Care+ adds 1 extra year of warranty. It can only be bought within 30 days of delivery.

7. PRICES
- Voltkart does not match prices from other websites.
- Support agents cannot give discounts, coupon codes or exceptions to this policy.

8. ORDERS
- To check an order, a refund or a delivery, the agent needs the order ID. Order IDs start with VK followed by 10 digits.

9. DAMAGED ON ARRIVAL
- Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the product and the package. Voltkart then sends a replacement.

10. SUPPORT HOURS
- Chat support: 24x7.
- Phone support: 9 am to 9 pm, every day.
- For anything this policy does not cover, offer to connect the customer to a human agent.
